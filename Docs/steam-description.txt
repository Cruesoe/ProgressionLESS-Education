[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3590863552]Progression: Education[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds an education system where you set up classrooms, schedule classes and have teachers train skills and proficiencies.
[*]Classes range from daycare for children to skill lessons, with training buildings for melee and shooting.
[*]Everything is managed from the Education tab.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Education adds up to [b]14 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 8[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Education[/b] and [b]Rough crib[/b] (Neolithic)
[*][b]Classrooms[/b] and [b]Training equipment[/b] (Medieval)
[*][b]Whiteboards[/b], [b]Electric classrooms[/b] and [b]Firearm station[/b] (Industrial)
[*][b]Spacer classrooms[/b] (Spacer)
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before. The Education tab is laid out as one clear teaching chain.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support:
[list]
[*]It works with Royalty, Biotech and Odyssey.
[*]It works with Medieval Overhaul, VFE Medieval 2, VFE Tribals, VFE Pirates and VFE Settlers.
[*]It works with Vanilla Cooking, Brewing, Furniture, Weapons, Psycasts, Vehicles and Gravship Expanded, VFE Farming and VFE Production.
[*]It works with VQE Ancients, Ancient Urban Ruins, Famine Food and Megafauna Bestiary.
[*]It works with Progression: Agriculture, Progression: Scenarios, and both Progression: Furniture and ProgressionLESS: Furniture.
[*]It works with Character Development, Character Creator, Better Architect, Node Research and Bradson's Main Button Icons.
[*]It works with several faction and scenario mods, including Samurai Faction, Satyr/Faun and Minotaur Expanded, Rimfather, Brave New Frontier, Orbital Station Start and Debt-Bound Gravship.
[*]Under Combat Extended, the training rifle, bow and charge rifle stay as vanilla training weapons.
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]

[b]Switching mid-game isn't recommended.[/b] Many of the original's research projects are merged, so swapping Progression: Education for this fork in an existing save can lose research progress. Start a new game when you switch.
