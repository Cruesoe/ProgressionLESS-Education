[h1]ProgressionLESS: Education[/h1]
[b]All the Progression goodness, less research.[/b]

[i]This is a fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3590863552]Progression: Education[/url] by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on. For a really good modpack, check out [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3521297585]The Progression Modpack[/url].[/i]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]Adds an education system: set up classrooms, schedule classes, and have teachers train skills and proficiencies.
[*]Classes range from daycare for children to skill lessons, with training buildings for melee and shooting.
[*]Everything is managed from the Education tab.
[/list]

[h2]Why "LESS"?[/h2]
Progression: Education adds up to [b]14 research projects[/b] depending on your mods. This fork groups them into [b]at most 8[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Neolithic:[/b] Education, Rough crib
[*][b]Medieval:[/b] Classrooms, Training equipment
[*][b]Industrial:[/b] Whiteboards, Electric classrooms, Firearm station
[*][b]Spacer:[/b] Spacer classrooms
[/list]
Everything stays in the same era, and still needs the other research it did before. The Education tab is laid out as one clear teaching chain.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Keeps all of the original's mod support, including:
[list]
[*]Royalty, Biotech and Odyssey
[*]Medieval Overhaul, VFE Medieval 2, VFE Tribals, VFE Pirates and VFE Settlers
[*]Vanilla Cooking, Brewing, Furniture, Weapons, Psycasts, Vehicles and Gravship Expanded, VFE Farming and VFE Production
[*]VQE Ancients, Ancient Urban Ruins, Famine Food and Megafauna Bestiary
[*]Progression: Agriculture, Progression: Scenarios, and both Progression: Furniture and ProgressionLESS: Furniture
[*]Character Development, Character Creator, Better Architect, Node Research and Bradson's Main Button Icons
[*]Several faction and scenario mods, including Samurai Faction, Satyr/Faun and Minotaur Expanded, Rimfather, Brave New Frontier, Orbital Station Start and Debt-Bound Gravship
[/list]

[b]Replaces the original. Don't use both.[/b]
